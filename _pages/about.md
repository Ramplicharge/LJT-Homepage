I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **2024–Present**: Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Supervisor: Prof. Junxian He.
- **2020–2024**: B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Graduated in June 2024.

## Research Interests

- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
